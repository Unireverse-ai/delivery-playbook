## Classify work before starting it

| Category | Decision test | Record required |
|---|---|---|
| Incident | Is the delivered workflow failing its agreed behaviour? | Impact, affected references, severity and owner |
| Correction | Does the implementation differ from an accepted requirement? | Requirement ID, evidence and correction scope |
| Included improvement | Is it within the explicitly agreed allowance? | Requested outcome, effort and allowance remaining |
| Scope change | Does it add or alter behaviour, data, systems or responsibilities? | Impact assessment and approval before implementation |

If classification is disputed, record the disagreement and agree a temporary handling decision. Do not silently convert an incident into chargeable new scope or assume all new requests are included.

## Periodic review and exit

At the agreed review point, compare observed quality, exception effort and operating cost against the baseline. Record proposed changes separately from measured results.

At exit, agree the final supported revision, export format, outstanding issues, ownership of artefacts, access revocation, deletion/retention steps and any transition support. Record completion in the [handover checklist](handover-checklist.md).
