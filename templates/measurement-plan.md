# Workflow outcome measurement plan · Template

**Use:** agree this plan before examining final pilot results. It defines how an outcome would be measured; no result is asserted by this template.

Project and accountable entity/team: ⟦name⟧ · Workflow/unit: ⟦for example: one eligible incoming enquiry⟧ · Baseline period: ⟦dates⟧ · Pilot period: ⟦dates⟧ · Plan revision/date: ⟦record⟧.

Business owner: ⟦role/name⟧ · Measurement owner: ⟦role/name⟧ · Client approver: ⟦role/name⟧.

## 1. Define the comparison

| Decision | Agreed method |
|---|---|
| Start and end of measured work | ⟦events, systems and included people⟧ |
| Included work and exclusions | ⟦eligibility, products, languages, formats and exceptions⟧ |
| Baseline source | ⟦timestamp records, time study, sampled observation or stated source⟧ |
| Counterfactual / comparison | ⟦concurrent control, phased rollout, matched historical cases or other method⟧ |
| Observation period | ⟦why the period covers normal operation, ramp-up and material cycles⟧ |
| Unrelated changes | ⟦volume, staffing, seasonality, case mix, pricing, other software/process changes⟧ |
| Attribution limits | ⟦what the design can and cannot attribute to this intervention⟧ |

A before/after difference may coincide with other changes. Record those changes and comparison limits instead of assigning the whole difference to AI.

## 2. Sample before selecting success stories

Sampling frame and selection: ⟦all eligible cases, random sample, consecutive dates, or justified alternative⟧. Sample size and rationale: ⟦number and precision/coverage reason⟧. Keep excluded, failed, abandoned and corrected cases visible.

| Case group | Expected share / importance | Required coverage | Actual coverage and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary eligible cases | ⟦basis⟧ | ⟦sample⟧ | ⟦not observed⟧ |
| Exceptions and ambiguous input | ⟦consequence / frequency⟧ | ⟦sample⟧ | ⟦not observed⟧ |
| Relevant languages, products or channels | ⟦groups⟧ | ⟦per-group sample⟧ | ⟦not observed⟧ |
| Failed / manually recovered cases | ⟦inclusion rule⟧ | ⟦all or specified sample⟧ | ⟦not observed⟧ |

Keep a held-out evaluation set where interpretation or model quality is assessed. Record who labelled it, how disagreements are resolved, and which examples were available during development. Report group-level results when an average could hide a material gap.

## 3. Measure effort, quality, adoption and spending separately

| Measure | Definition and denominator | Baseline | Pilot result / period | Evidence and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Handling effort | Active human minutes across included roles / same unit of work | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦source⟧ |
| Review and exceptions | Review, correction, escalation and recovery time; state whether already in handling minutes | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦source⟧ |
| Turnaround | Elapsed time between defined events; business/calendar time and summary statistics | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦source⟧ |
| Quality | Correct/complete outcomes and consequential errors / case denominator | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦labels/review⟧ |
| Adoption | Cases using the workflow / eligible cases; record abandonment | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦usage⟧ |
| Operating cash cost | Incremental model, hosting, integration, monitoring, maintenance and paid oversight for the period | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦account records⟧ |
| Cash reduction | Actual spending change linked to an identified mechanism, such as purchased hours or overtime | ⟦value⟧ | ⟦not measured⟧ | ⟦spending record and other causes⟧ |

Report recovered hours as capacity and state what happens to it. Reduced handling time does not establish a payroll reduction. Include one-time implementation, migration and training costs in the investment assessment; record when paid. Avoid counting review time in both per-item handling and additional oversight.

Use one currency and consistent periods. Show before/after absolute values, denominator and formula for any percentage. Distinguish estimates, projections and observations. Report dispersion, sample size and an uncertainty method where appropriate rather than treating a point estimate as certain.

## 4. Decide and preserve the record

Quality/operating constraints required for a value claim: ⟦thresholds and owners⟧.

Unresolved evidence or confounders: ⟦item, impact, owner and next observation⟧.

Decision: ⟦proceed / narrow / observe longer / stop⟧ · Client approval of interpretation: ⟦owner, revision and date⟧.

Permission to publish is separate: ⟦permitted wording/data/names, authorising party and date, or not granted⟧. Keep raw samples and customer records private, with retention/access defined in the [data record](data-and-suppliers.md).

Next: carry the evidence into the [pilot scorecard](pilot-evaluation-scorecard.md), [value model](https://github.com/UniReverseAI/ai-value-assessment) and, when supported and permitted, the [case study](case-study.md).

Method reference: [NIST AI RMF 1.0, validity and reliability](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/3-sec-characteristics/) connects accuracy evidence with representative test conditions and documented methods. Business measurement choices must be agreed for the particular workflow.
