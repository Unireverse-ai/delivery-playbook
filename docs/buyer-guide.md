# A five-minute buyer review

Start with the question your role needs to answer. Each route points to something inspectable and identifies the evidence needed for a real engagement.

**Public evidence:** synthetic reference code, reproducible calculations and delivery specimens. These materials do not establish customer results, production operation or independent certification. Keep contracts, personal credentials, customer records and environment details in an agreed private review channel.

## Procurement

| Question | Inspect publicly | Establish privately before engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Who is the contracting party and who owns the decisions? | [Official company register](https://ariregister.rik.ee/eng/company/17634890), [company contact](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai#company-and-contact), and [pilot roles](pilot-brief.md#3-actors-and-authority) | Confirm the contracting entity, authorised representatives, assigned project owners and the experience relevant to the work. Registration does not establish technical ability or customer results. |
| Where would our data go, and who can change that? | [Data and supplier record](../templates/data-and-suppliers.md) | Obtain the completed data flow, exact services/account configurations, permissions, retention decisions and the person who verified each supplier. |
| What do we receive, and how do we leave or change the arrangement? | [Handover](../templates/handover-checklist.md), [support/change scope](../templates/support-and-change.md), and [rights](../RIGHTS.md) | Agree deliverables, ownership and permitted use, source/access handover, support coverage, dependencies, export and exit responsibilities. Public reference visibility does not define the rights in a client deliverable. |

## Business

| Question | Inspect publicly | Establish before investing in the next stage |
|---|---|---|
| Is the first workflow narrow enough to evaluate? | [Worked pilot brief](pilot-brief.md); [requirements](../templates/requirements.md) | Agree one unit of work, included products/systems/languages, excluded actions and the owner of the baseline. |
| Is the claimed value capacity, cash, or a projection? | [Value model](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/ai-value-assessment), [monthly cash-flow method](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/ai-value-assessment/blob/main/docs/cashflow.md), and [measurement plan](../templates/measurement-plan.md) | Obtain before/after effort, review and exception costs, adoption, the actual spending mechanism and a comparison that accounts for volume and case mix. |
| What would make us proceed, narrow scope, or stop? | [Pilot scorecard](../templates/pilot-evaluation-scorecard.md) | Set thresholds, required evidence and decision owners before seeing final results. Keep technical acceptance, economic acceptance and permission to go live separate. |

## Technical

| Question | Inspect publicly | Establish in the proposed environment |
|---|---|---|
| Can we reproduce the implementation and its checks? | [Run the workflow reference](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/sales-workflow-reference#run-the-evidence); [control acceptance](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/sales-workflow-reference/blob/main/docs/acceptance.md) | Match the revision, configuration, inputs and report. Inspect failed cases as well as passing tests; a successful run is bounded by those inputs and that environment. |
| Does the extraction evidence match the intended input? | [Evaluation method](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/sales-workflow-reference/blob/main/docs/evaluation.md), [synthetic dataset card](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/sales-workflow-reference/blob/main/evaluations/DATASET_CARD.md), and [actual parser baseline](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/sales-workflow-reference/blob/main/evaluations/baseline-report.md) | Establish representative languages/formats, held-out cases, field errors, clarification decisions, unsupported inputs and provider settings if a model is used. The published synthetic parser baseline is not a language-model or customer benchmark. |
| Can an incorrect or repeated action be contained and recovered? | [Architecture](https://github.com/Unireverse-ai/sales-workflow-reference/blob/main/docs/architecture.md); [runbook](../templates/operations-runbook.md) | Verify authenticated authority, downstream identifiers, duplicate and unknown-outcome handling, recovery exercises, monitoring and operating ownership. The local scripted review is not authentication. |
